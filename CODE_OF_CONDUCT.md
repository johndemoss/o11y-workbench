# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes contributions from anyone. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful of differing experience levels, viewpoints, and backgrounds.
- Give and accept technical criticism about the work, not the person.
- Ask questions plainly and answer them patiently.
- Respect a maintainer's decision once it is made, even when disagreeing with it.
- Credit other people's work and follow the licenses in `NOTICE` and `LICENSE`.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discriminatory comments,
sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, review comments, commit messages, and any
other project space, and to project participants acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include credentials, captured telemetry, or personal data in a report.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of this policy.

For conduct problems, use GitHub's reporting tools: the kebab menu on the
offending issue, pull request, or comment offers **Report content**, and a
user's profile offers **Block or report**. GitHub receives and reviews those
reports; there is no separate project conduct inbox.

GitHub may also act under its own [Community
Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines)
and [Terms of
Service](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service).
Maintainers may comment, lock, hide, or remove content, or block accounts, to
keep project spaces usable.
